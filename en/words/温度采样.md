A decoding method that divides the [[logits]] $z$ of a [[language model]] by a temperature $T > 0$, applies [[softmax]], and draws the next [[token]] at random from the resulting distribution. A temperature $T < 1$ concentrates the distribution, which approaches [[greedy decoding]] as $T \to 0$; a temperature $T > 1$ flattens it, making the output more varied.

Example: Let $z = (2, 1, 0)$. With $T = 1$ the probabilities are about $(0.67, 0.24, 0.09)$; with $T = 0.5$, about $(0.87, 0.12, 0.02)$; with $T = 2$, about $(0.51, 0.31, 0.19)$.
