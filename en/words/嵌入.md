A table lookup that maps discrete [[token|tokens]] to continuous vectors. With vocabulary $\mathcal{V}$ and embedding matrix $E \in \mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{V}| \times d}$, the token with index $t$ maps to row $t$ of $E$. The matrix $E$ is trained together with the rest of the network.

Example: With the vocabulary “cat”, “dog”, “fish” in that order and $d = 2$,

$$E = \begin{pmatrix} 0.2 & 0.9 \\ 0.3 & 0.8 \\ 0.9 & 0.1 \end{pmatrix},$$

and “dog”, with index 2, maps to $(0.3, 0.8)$.
