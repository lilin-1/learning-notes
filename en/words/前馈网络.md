A [[neural network]] in which information flows only from input to output, with no cycles.

Example: The two-layer feedforward network

$$f(x) = \max(0,\, xW_1 + b_1)\,W_2 + b_2$$

uses ReLU as its [[activation function]]; $W_1$ and $W_2$ are weight matrices, $b_1$ and $b_2$ are biases. With $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{512 \times 2048}$ and $W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{2048 \times 512}$, it has about 2.1 million parameters.
