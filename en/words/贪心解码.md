A decoding method that picks, at every step, the [[token]] to which the [[language model]] assigns the highest probability, until an end-of-sequence token is generated. [[softmax|Softmax]] preserves order, so this is also the token with the largest [[logits|logit]]. The method is simple and deterministic, but choosing the best token at each step does not guarantee the most probable sequence.

Example: At the first step $P(a) = 0.5$ and $P(b) = 0.4$. After $a$, the highest next probability is $0.4$; after $b$, it reaches $0.9$. Greedy decoding yields a two-token sequence of probability $0.5 \times 0.4 = 0.2$, whereas a sequence starting with $b$ reaches $0.4 \times 0.9 = 0.36$.
