A method that extends the context by scaling positions beyond the training length back into the trained range and then fine-tuning for a few steps. With training length $L$ and target length $L'$, position $m$ is fed to [[rotary position embedding]] as $mL / L'$, so every rotation angle falls within the range seen in training.

Note: Extrapolating directly instead, the model meets rotation angles it has never seen, its attention scores run out of control and [[perplexity]] soars. The price of interpolation is that adjacent positions differ by smaller angles, slightly weakening the model's ability to tell nearby order apart, which fine-tuning must adjust for. The original paper extended LLaMA's context to 32768 with fewer than 1000 fine-tuning steps.

Example: To extend a training length of 2048 to 8192, the scale factor is $2048 / 8192 = 1/4$. Position 6000 is fed as 1500, still within the trained range; direct extrapolation would face position 6000, never seen before.

Paper: [Extending Context Window of Large Language Models via Positional Interpolation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595) (Chen et al., 2023)
