A training objective in which the model predicts several following tokens at once at every position. Gloeckle and colleagues place $n$ output heads side by side on a shared trunk, predicting tokens $t + 1$ to $t + n$. DeepSeek-V3 uses chained modules instead: the $k$-th module takes the representation from the previous depth and the embedding of token $t + k$, and predicts one token further, keeping the full causal chain.

Note: Predicting several steps ahead in training forces the model to plan, and it learns better representations; in the original paper, a 13-billion-parameter model solved 12% more problems on HumanEval. At inference the extra heads or modules can be discarded, or serve as drafts for [[speculative decoding]]: they share the embedding and output head with the main model and guess better than a separate small model.

Example: DeepSeek-V3 predicts just 1 extra token. Used for speculative decoding, this token is accepted 85% to 90% of the time, and generation runs 1.8 times as fast.

Paper: [Better & Faster Large Language Models via Multi-token Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19737) (Gloeckle et al., 2024)
