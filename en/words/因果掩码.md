A mask in [[self-attention]] that stops each position from attending to the positions after it. Before [[softmax]], every element $(i, j)$ with $j > i$ of the score matrix $QK^{\top}/\sqrt{d_k}$ of [[scaled dot-product attention]] is set to $-\infty$, so its weight becomes $0$. The output at position $i$ then depends only on positions $1, \dots, i$; in training, all positions can be computed in parallel without leaking the [[token]] to be predicted.

Example: With $n = 3$ and all scores equal to $0$, the masked weight matrix is

$$\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1/2 & 1/2 & 0 \\ 1/3 & 1/3 & 1/3 \end{pmatrix}.$$
