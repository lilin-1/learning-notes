The time between two consecutive output [[token|tokens]] in streaming output, abbreviated ITL; its average is also called time per output token (TPOT). It is set by the duration of each [[prefill and decode|decode]] step and depends on the batch size and the memory bandwidth.

Example: With an inter-token latency of 20 milliseconds, 50 tokens are output per second. A 500-token answer takes about 10 seconds, plus the [[time to first token]].
