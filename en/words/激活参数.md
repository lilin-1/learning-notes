The parameters of a [[mixture of experts]] model that take part in processing one [[token]]. They comprise the $k$ selected experts and the parts every token passes through, such as the attention layers, the embedding layer and any shared experts, which every token uses. Their counterpart is the total parameter count: all experts plus the shared parts.

Note: The two govern different things. Total parameters determine memory: all experts usually have to stay resident, since there is no telling which ones the next token will select. Active parameters determine computation: the forward pass for one token takes about twice as many floating-point operations as there are active parameters. So a model with 1 trillion total and 32 billion active parameters needs about 1 TB of GPU memory even at 8 bits, while its computation per token is close to that of a dense 32-billion-parameter model.

Example: DeepSeek-V3 has 671 billion parameters, about 37 billion of them active per token; Kimi K2 has about 1 trillion, with about 32 billion active. In the name Qwen3-235B-A22B, A22B means 22 billion active parameters.
