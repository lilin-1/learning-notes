The phenomenon in which the first few [[token|tokens]] of a sequence absorb a large share of the attention weights. The weights of [[softmax]] must sum to 1, so attention has to land somewhere even when no position deserves it. The first tokens are seen by almost every later position and become that landing place, even if they carry little meaning.

Note: Building on this, StreamingLLM keeps only the first 4 tokens and the most recent stretch in the [[KV cache]], discarding the middle. The model can then run stably on streaming input for millions of tokens. A plain sliding window, once it pushes the first tokens out of the cache, sees perplexity soar. The method lets a model keep running, but not remember what was discarded.

Example: On Llama 2, StreamingLLM uses a cache of 2048 positions: the first 4 plus the latest 2044. Compared with recomputing a window at every step, it is up to 22.2 times faster.
