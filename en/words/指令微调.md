Continuing to train a pretrained [[language model]] on many tasks described by instructions, also called supervised fine-tuning (SFT). Each example is an instruction with the desired response, the loss is still the per-token [[cross-entropy]], and it is usually computed only on the tokens of the response.

Note: A pretrained model only continues text and does not necessarily answer: asked a question, it may go on to write more questions. Instruction tuning teaches it the format and habits of answering instructions, with far less data than pretraining; the model's knowledge still comes mainly from pretraining.

Example: FLAN fine-tuned a 137-billion-parameter model on more than 60 tasks rewritten with instruction templates. Zero-shot, it beat the 175-billion-parameter GPT-3 on 20 of 25 tasks.

Paper: [Finetuned Language Models Are Zero-Shot Learners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01652) (Wei et al., 2021)
