A function that maps a model's output and its target to a real number, smaller when the two are closer. Training searches for the parameters that minimize its average over the training data.

Example: The mean squared error $L = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$, where $y$ is the target and $\hat{y}$ the output. With target $(1, 2)$ and output $(1.5, 1)$, $L = (0.25 + 1) / 2 = 0.625$.
