The quantity $H(q, p) = -\sum_i q_i \ln p_i$ between two probability distributions $q$ and $p$, which, as a function of $p$, is smallest at $p = q$. As a classification [[loss function]], $p$ is the class distribution output by the model, usually obtained from the [[logits]] through [[softmax]], and $q$ is $1$ at the true class $c$ and $0$ elsewhere, so the loss is $-\ln p_c$.

Example: $p = (0.7, 0.2, 0.1)$. If the true class is the first, the loss is $-\ln 0.7 \approx 0.36$; if it is the third, $-\ln 0.1 \approx 2.30$. The more confidently wrong the model, the larger the loss.
