The [[attention mechanism]] in which the queries, keys and values all come from the same sequence. If the sequence is represented by $X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$, then

$$Q = XW^Q, \quad K = XW^K, \quad V = XW^V,$$

where $W^Q$, $W^K$ and $W^V$ are learned matrices. Each position's output is a weighted sum of the values at all positions, so any two positions are only one step apart; the price is computation that grows as $n^2$.

Example: Two tokens are represented by $x_1 = (1, 0)$ and $x_2 = (0, 1)$; take $W^Q = W^K = W^V = I$ and dot-product scores. Position 1 has scores $(1, 0)$, weights about $(0.73, 0.27)$ and output $(0.73, 0.27)$, which mixes in information from position 2.
