An algorithm that computes the gradient of the [[loss function]] with respect to every parameter, layer by layer from output to input, by the chain rule. One forward pass and one backward pass yield all the gradients, at a cost of the same order as the forward pass; the gradients are then used by [[gradient descent]].

Example: $y = (wx)^2$ with $x = 3$ and $w = 2$. Forward: $u = wx = 6$, $y = u^2 = 36$. Backward: $\partial y / \partial u = 2u = 12$, $\partial y / \partial w = 12 \cdot x = 36$, which matches the direct derivative $2wx^2 = 36$.
