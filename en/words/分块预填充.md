A scheduling method that cuts the [[prefill and decode|prefill]] of a long prompt into chunks and runs them in the same batches as the decode steps of other requests. When a whole prefill runs in one go, the requests decoding in the same batch must wait for it, and the [[inter-token latency]] spikes. With chunks, the computation in each step has an upper bound, and decoding requests advance at every step.

Note: The chunk size trades one latency against another. Small chunks keep each step short and the inter-token latency smooth; large chunks finish long prompts sooner, lowering the [[time to first token]] and raising throughput.

Example: The token budget per step is 512, and 32 requests decode 1 [[token]] each. A 4000-token prompt can prefill at most $512 - 32 = 480$ tokens per step and finishes in $\lceil 4000 / 480 \rceil = 9$ steps, during which the 32 decoding requests keep producing output at every step.
