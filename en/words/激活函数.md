A nonlinear function $\phi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ applied elementwise between two affine maps. Without it, a composition of affine maps is still affine: $(xW_1 + b_1)W_2 + b_2 = x(W_1W_2) + (b_1W_2 + b_2)$, so any number of layers collapses into one.

Example: ReLU, $\phi(x) = \max(0, x)$. Applied elementwise to $(-2, 0.5, 3)$, it gives $(0, 0.5, 3)$.
