The [[attention mechanism]] in which the queries come from one sequence and the keys and values from another. With the two sequences represented by $X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times d}$ and $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$, $Q = XW^Q$, $K = YW^K$ and $V = YW^V$; the output has $n$ rows, each a weighted sum of the values at the positions of $Y$.

Example: In English-to-Chinese translation, the queries come from the 3 tokens the [[decoder]] has generated, and the keys and values from the outputs of the [[encoder]] for 5 English tokens. The weight matrix is $3 \times 5$; row $i$ holds the weights that decoder position $i$ assigns to the positions of the source sentence.
