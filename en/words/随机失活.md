A regularization method that, in training, sets each unit's output to $0$ independently with probability $p$ and divides the remaining outputs by $1 - p$; inference leaves the outputs unchanged. Dividing by $1 - p$ keeps each unit's expected output the same in training and in inference. The method forces a [[neural network]] not to rely on individual units, which reduces overfitting.

Example: Let $p = 0.5$ and a layer output $(2, 4, 6, 8)$. If units 1 and 3 happen to be kept, the output becomes $(4, 0, 12, 0)$; averaged over all possible choices, the expected output is still $(2, 4, 6, 8)$. The original [[Transformer]] takes $p = 0.1$.

Paper: [Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting](https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html) (Srivastava et al., 2014)
