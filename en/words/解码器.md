The part of a [[neural network]] that generates the output from an intermediate representation. When generating a sequence, the decoder produces [[token|tokens]] one at a time, each step conditioned on the tokens generated before it; in an encoder–decoder architecture it also reads the output of the [[encoder]].

Example: When the output is “the cat sleeps”, the decoder emits “the”, “cat”, “sleeps” and an end-of-sequence token in turn; the step that emits “sleeps” is conditioned on “the” and “cat”.
