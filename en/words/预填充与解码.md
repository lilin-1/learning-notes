The two phases of [[language model]] inference. Prefill processes all the [[token|tokens]] of the prompt at once, computing their keys and values in parallel, writing them to the [[KV cache]] and producing the first output token. Decode then processes one new token per step, reading the cache to produce the next. Prefill handles many tokens at once, has high [[arithmetic intensity]] and is limited by compute; decode does little computation per step yet must read all the parameters and the cache, and is limited by memory bandwidth.

Example: A 7-billion-parameter model stored in 16-bit floating point takes about 14 GB, and every decode step must read it from GPU memory at least once. On a GPU with 2 TB/s of bandwidth, a single sequence can generate at most about $2000 / 14 \approx 143$ tokens per second, almost regardless of compute.
