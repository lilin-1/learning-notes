A subword tokenization algorithm, abbreviated BPE, that starts from single characters and repeatedly merges the most frequent pair of adjacent symbols in the corpus into a new symbol. The symbols obtained after a number of merges form the vocabulary of [[token|tokens]]. Common words end up as single tokens and rare words split into several subwords; as long as the base symbols include every character or byte, any text can be tokenized.

Example: The corpus is low, lower, now. The pair o, w occurs 3 times, more than any other, and is merged first into ow; then l, ow occurs 2 times, the most, and is merged into low. So lower is split into low, e, r, and now into n, ow.

Paper: [Neural Machine Translation of Rare Words with Subword Units](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.07909) (Sennrich et al., 2015)
