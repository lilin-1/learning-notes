# What can be done when a model does not fit in GPU memory?

There are two kinds of remedy: make the model smaller, or spread it over several GPUs. [[quantization|Quantization]] lowers the weights from 16 bits to 8 or 4, cutting their size to $1/2$ or $1/4$. [[tensor parallelism|Tensor parallelism]] splits the matrices of each layer across GPUs and suits GPUs with very high bandwidth within one machine; [[pipeline parallelism]] divides the model by layers, communicates little and suits spanning machines.

Besides the weights, the [[KV cache]] also takes a great deal of memory. [[grouped-query attention|Grouped-query attention]] shrinks it through the structure of the model, and [[PagedAttention]] removes the waste of reserving it for the maximum length.

Example: A 70-billion-parameter model takes about 140 GB in 16 bits and does not fit on an 80 GB GPU. Quantized to 4 bits it takes about 35 GB and fits on one GPU; alternatively, unquantized, it can be split across 4 GPUs with tensor parallelism, about 35 GB each.
