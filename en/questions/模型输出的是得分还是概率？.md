# Does a model output logits or probabilities?

Logits; probabilities are computed from them by [[softmax]]. The output layer of a [[language model]] multiplies the last layer's vector at the current position by a matrix, obtaining the [[logits]] for every [[token]] in the vocabulary at once. “The model outputs probabilities” is shorthand for “it outputs logits that then pass through softmax”.

Finding the most probable token therefore needs no search: all the probabilities are computed at once, and the largest is taken. Softmax preserves order, so [[greedy decoding]] can take the largest logit directly, with the same result. To change the shape of the distribution, one works on the logits: [[temperature sampling]] divides them by a temperature before softmax, and the [[cross-entropy]] in training is also computed directly from them.

Example: Suppose the vocabulary has only three tokens, with logits $(2, 1, 0)$ and probabilities of about $(0.67, 0.24, 0.09)$; greedy decoding picks the first. Divided by a temperature of 2, the probabilities become about $(0.51, 0.31, 0.19)$. The smallest GPT-2 produces 50257 logits at each step.
