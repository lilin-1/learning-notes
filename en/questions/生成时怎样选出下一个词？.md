# How is the next word chosen during generation?

There are three kinds of method, all starting from the distribution over the next [[token]] given by the [[language model]]; they differ only in how they use it: take the maximum, search, or sample.

[[greedy decoding|Greedy decoding]] takes the most probable token at each step; the result is deterministic but often bland and repetitive. [[beam search|Beam search]] keeps several candidates at once to find sequences of higher overall probability, which suits tasks with a correct answer, such as translation. [[temperature sampling|Temperature sampling]] draws at random from the distribution, with the temperature setting how random, which suits conversation and writing, where variety is wanted.

Example: With logits $(2, 1, 0)$, greedy decoding always picks the first token. At temperature 1, the three tokens are chosen with probabilities of about $(0.67, 0.24, 0.09)$; at temperature 2, about $(0.51, 0.31, 0.19)$.
