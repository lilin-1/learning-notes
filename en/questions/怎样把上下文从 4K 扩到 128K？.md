# How can the context be extended from 4K to 128K?

Three things must be solved: the positional encoding must recognize farther positions, attention must remain computable, and the cache must fit.

For position, [[rotary position embedding]] meets rotation angles never seen beyond the training length, and direct extrapolation breaks down. [[position interpolation|Position interpolation]] scales distant positions back into the trained range; [[YaRN]] goes further, interpolating only the slowly rotating dimensions and adjusting the attention temperature. Both need only a few fine-tuning steps on long texts.

For computation, [[FlashAttention]] spares attention from writing out the $n \times n$ matrix, and in training [[context parallelism]] spreads long sequences over several GPUs. At inference the [[KV cache]] grows linearly with the length and can be shrunk with [[grouped-query attention]] or [[multi-head latent attention]].

Example: DeepSeek-V3 used YaRN in two stages to extend its context from 4K to 32K and then to 128K, training only 1000 steps in each stage.
